Can Jack help me with…?

Don't take my word for it. Ask your own AI. This skill file gives it my experience, real numbers and honest gaps, plus rules that stop it from overselling me. You'll get a straight verdict, including "not his area".

Try:

    

    
Paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any assistant that can read links. If it can't, open the file and paste its contents instead.

  






  
What you'll get

  
Four possible verdicts

  

    
✅
He has done this
A specific case where he owned this work and its result, with numbers.

    
🟡
Adjacent
He solved a structurally similar problem elsewhere. You'll see what transfers.

    
🟠
Stretch
Some parts transfer, the core is new. You'll see exactly which parts.

    
❌
Not his area
Said plainly. Paid media and traditional CPG S&OP, for example.

  






  
Example

  
What an answer looks like

  

    
Can Jack help me with our operating processes and P&L analysis?

    

      
🟡 Partly yes. Processes: ✅. P&L analysis: 🟡 adjacent.

      
    
        
  • He has redesigned operations end to end, e.g. a 4-hour daily reconciliation brought to ~3 minutes, which avoided a hire [C3], and documented a whole company's processes so anyone can take them over [C6].
    • 
        
  • He owned a division's P&L as it grew from zero to a standalone revenue line [C2], and built the invoice and supplier-credit visibility Finance uses for working-capital projections [C3].
    • 
        
  • Gap: he isn't a finance specialist. He hasn't run FP&A cycles, a month-end close or an audit.
    • 
      

      
Likely approach: map how your numbers are produced today, fix the operational data feeding them, and pair with your finance lead on the analysis.
