The nightly job that could erase weeks of sales work

At Rafónica, robots measure creators every night and a person builds every relationship. Store both in one "status" field and the robot overwrites the person. So every record has two states that never mix: the data, owned by automation, and the relationship, owned by a human.

My startup

What Rafónica does

Rafónica connects nano and micro content creators with local businesses that have never done influencer marketing, so both sides know exactly what they are buying and selling: real reach instead of follower counts, clear agreements instead of DMs, and consent before anyone is offered to a brand.

The system

Two pipelines that meet at one point

Supply · creators
  1. Discover candidates
  2. Capture and measure
  3. Find contact channels
  4. Refresh on a schedule
  5. Outreach
  6. Consent and signed agreement
Demand · businesses
  1. Discover businesses from open data
  2. Enrich and find who decides
  3. Outreach
  4. Interested business
Both meet at matchingA proposal pairs a business with signed creators whose audience fits. Then the campaign runs, and the same scheduled snapshots measure the result.
Architecture

The scraper isn't the asset. The directory is.

Capture layer

Disposable and isolated behind one interface. Platforms change and capture breaks: when it does, one file gets repaired, not the system.

Intelligence layer

The real asset: reach scoring, locality, niche, contactability, matching, history over time, detection of bought followers.

Business layer

A self-hosted database: the creator directory, candidates, snapshots and deals, plus a separate CRM of businesses, contacts and interactions.

Rules between processes

So automation never overwrites people

01

Scripts never write a relationship

Only a person, or an approved queue, moves a creator or a business through the conversation.

02

People never write the data state

If a measurement is wrong, fix the script or leave a note. Don't fake the state.

03

Enrichment never overwrites

Automatic contact discovery only fills empty fields. A contact verified by hand always wins.

04

Privacy first, every time

Every intake and capture checks the exclusion list before writing anything.

05

Fail closed

If the exclusion list can't be read, nothing runs. Better a stopped pipeline than re-contacting someone who asked to leave.

06

Consent is the inventory

Nobody is offered to a business without a signed agreement, however good their numbers look.
