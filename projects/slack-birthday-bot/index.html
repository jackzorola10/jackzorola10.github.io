    Design decisions

    Small rules, big difference

    📅

    Weekends move to Friday

    And the message names the real date. Nobody gets a party in an empty channel.

    🔁

    Fair rotation

    Least-used message and GIF first, ties at random. Still fresh after a year.

    🕰️

    "Today" only on the day

    Messages that say "today" are excluded from Friday and belated posts.

    🩹

    Self-recovery

    A failed day is caught up on the next weekday with a belated note, up to 4 days back.

    🔑

    Daily token check

    auth.test runs every weekday, so a revoked token fails on a quiet Tuesday.

    🧾

    Post first, log second

    If Slack fails, nothing is logged and the next run retries. No duplicates, no silent gaps.
